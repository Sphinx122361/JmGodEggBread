About Us

JM God Egg Bread

JM God Egg Bread is a student-made food business that offers EggyBite, a delicious and affordable snack made with soft bread, creamy egg and mayonnaise filling, and a crispy bread crumb coating.

Our goal is to provide students, teachers, and families with a tasty and affordable snack made from quality ingredients. We also aim to develop our skills in entrepreneurship, teamwork, and customer service.

Objectives

  • To produce a tasty egg bread snack.
  • To offer affordable snacks for students and instructors.
  • To develop a unique egg bread product.
  • To satisfy customers through quality service and tasty snacks.
  • To promote a homemade Japanese-inspired snack.
