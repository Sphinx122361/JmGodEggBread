Description

EggyBite is a homemade snack made from tasty bread filled with a creamy egg and mayonnaise mixture. It is coated with bread crumbs and fried until golden and crispy. With its soft bread, rich filling, and crunchy outer layer, EggyBite is a simple yet delicious snack for students, teachers, and families.

Objective of Product Selling

  • To provide an affordable and delicious snack that students and customers can enjoy.
  • To offer a creative egg-based product that is different from ordinary bread snacks.
  • To satisfy customers by using quality and fresh ingredients.
  • To earn profit while keeping the product reasonably priced.

Product Procedure

  1. Prepare all the ingredients needed for EggyBite.
  2. Prepare the egg filling by mixing the egg, mayonnaise, and powdered pepper.
  3. Flatten the bread and add the egg mixture inside.
  4. Shape and seal the bread to prevent the filling from coming out.
  5. Coat the prepared bread with beaten egg and bread crumbs.
  6. Fry until golden brown and crispy.
  7. Allow the product to cool before serving.
  8. Give samples to selected testers, such as students and teachers.
  9. Collect feedback about taste, texture, appearance, and overall experience.

Ingredients

  1. Mayonnaise
  2. Powdered pepper
  3. Egg
  4. Bread crumbs
  5. Tasty bread
  6. Oil
